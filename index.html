Settings

Download Quality

Lower quality = less internet data used. Songs you already downloaded stay as they are.

Add from URL

For tracks not on JioSaavn (e.g. niche YouTube uploads): use a working YouTube → MP3 converter like ezmp3.to or onlymp3.co, copy the resulting MP3 link, and paste it above. Most direct .mp3 URLs from mdundo.com and afrocharts.com also work here.

Import from device

Cloud Sync

Sign in to automatically back up your library (songs list, playlists, favorites — no audio) to your account. On a new device, sign in and it pulls your library back and re-downloads the songs.

Not signed in

Signed in as

Library Sync

Save a small file of your library (songs list, playlists, favorites — no audio). If you reinstall, import it to rebuild your library and auto re-download the songs.

Import a Song List

Paste songs, one per line (e.g. Israel Mbonyi - Nina Siri). Copy a list from Spotify, WhatsApp, anywhere. MusiMe finds each song and downloads it for offline.

Backup & Restore

Full backup includes the audio files (large). Use this to move everything to another device without re-downloading.

Storage

Calculating...

Add to Home Screen for reliable offline playback. Browser tabs can lose stored songs after inactivity.

Playback Diagnostics

If lock-screen play/pause misbehaves, reproduce it on your phone, then tap Show Log and share the output.
